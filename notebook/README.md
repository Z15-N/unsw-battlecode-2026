# Working notes (unedited)

These are the live documents the project ran on, kept as they were on the last day (Oct 13, 2026). They were written
as running instructions and a lab notebook for the AI coding agent that implemented most of the code, so they are
dense, dated and full of internal shorthand.

- [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md): what the bot does and why, every experiment with its numbers, and the queue of ideas.
- [STRATEGY_archive.md](STRATEGY_archive.md): older analyses moved out of STRATEGY.md.
- [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md): the loop every version followed (analyse, decide, build, gate, publish, track).
- [GOALS.md](GOALS.md): the goals and the release checklist.

Other teams are named here because their public replays were studied. "Copied from X" means a behaviour measured in
their replays and re-implemented, not their code.

For a readable summary, start with the [README](../README.md) and [docs/](../docs/).
