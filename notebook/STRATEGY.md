## 1. How a game is won
1. Our queen alive and theirs dead: **we win**. With our queen alive we win ~93% of ranked games (79 of 88, v46–v52).
2. Both queens alive: the longer queen wins. Feed her late.
3. Both dead: longest dragon, then total length.
4. Elimination ends the game early.

A death drops ceil(L/2) pearls (2 → 1, 3 → 2, 10 → 5). Dragons must act every turn. Queens are ids 0 and 1 and move first.
